Steven Braun

I’m a research scientist working on deep neural networks and probabilistic models. I did my PhD in the Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Lab at TU Darmstadt and focused on bridging probabilistic circuits and deep neural networks. Outside of research, I love writing software, tinkering with Linux, macOS, Emacs, and my self-hosting homelab. I also play badminton and co-lead the badminton department of my local sports club.
